# Code of conduct

OpenCortyx follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull
requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/opencortyx/OpenCortyx/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
